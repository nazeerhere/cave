Purpose
-------
The original 4 presentation pages have been separated into action-specific,
label-free sprite strips so Codex/Unity can process each animation independently.

- Idle: 8 frames
- Walk: 8 frames
- Fireball Cast: 8 frames
- Laser Charge: 8 frames
- Laser Release: 7 frames
- Hurt: 6 frames

CORRUPTED GOTH

Notes
-----
- Character designs were not regenerated or redesigned.
- These are crops/repacked strips from the supplied approved source pages.
- Presentation-page titles, action labels, frame numbers, and page borders are excluded.
- Background remains the source dark presentation background; Unity/Codex can perform
  the final transparent runtime crop/import step without having to parse the original pages.
